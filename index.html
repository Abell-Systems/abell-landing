The problem

The next opportunity is rarely visible in a single dataset.

Innovation intelligence is fragmented across scientific literature, patents, companies, technologies, products and market signals.

Existing tools can help organizations search these sources individually. The problem is identifying meaningful relationships between them — especially relationships that have not yet become obvious opportunities.

A white space is more than an empty market.

An apparent gap does not necessarily represent an opportunity.

Abell looks for evidence across multiple technological and market signals to distinguish unexplored spaces from areas where technology, demand or intellectual property are already sufficiently developed.

Our goal is to identify evidence-backed opportunities emerging between existing knowledge, technology and demand.

The intelligence layer behind Abell.

Nexus is the research infrastructure behind Abell's innovation intelligence. It provides the mechanisms for structuring, connecting and analysing heterogeneous technology and knowledge sources.

Today, Nexus demonstrates semantic retrieval from real technology demand to Spanish patents and utility models. Next, the platform extends this foundation toward multi-source relationship modelling and white-space discovery.

Vision • Mens et Manus

A map of where technology can go next.

The long-term vision of Abell is an intelligence layer connecting scientific knowledge, technology, intellectual property and market demand at scale.

As these relationships become richer, Abell can move from finding existing technologies to identifying where new technological opportunities are emerging.

Abell began at MIT in Boston, from a simple observation: scientific breakthroughs were being created faster than they could reach the people and problems they could help.
